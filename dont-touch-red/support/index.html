We can help.

Support for close calls and suspiciously red screens.

How do I play?

Drag the small white character with your thumb and survive without touching any object that is actually red. Background flashes, labels, and other distractions do not count as collisions.

Why did the controls change?

Higher-scoring runs introduce tricks such as reversed controls, rotation, grayscale, color transitions, fake alerts, and changing character size. These are intentional challenge states.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone receives the same deterministic challenge for the day. Your first official result is saved on the device; Endless mode remains available afterward.

Why didn’t a rewarded revive work?

A revive is granted only after the rewarded ad finishes and the ad provider confirms the reward. If an ad is unavailable or closed early, return to the game-over screen and try a new run.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen the app if audio, haptics, or controls stop responding.
  • Check Settings to confirm motion, sound, music, haptics, and color assist are enabled as expected.
  • Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling if a completed Remove Ads purchase is not recognized.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, game mode, score, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording is helpful.
