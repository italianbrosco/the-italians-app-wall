What the app stores

Your settings, selected character, unlocked cosmetics, daily result, best score, and active run state are stored locally on your device. Don’t Touch Red does not require an account.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to offer an optional rewarded revive and occasional ads. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, Apple or Google processes the transaction through your store account. Italian Bros does not receive your full payment-card details. The app stores the resulting entitlement on your device and can ask the store to restore it.

Sharing

If you choose to share a daily result, your device opens its standard share sheet with a score summary. You decide which service receives it and whether to complete the action.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes locally stored game data, subject to your device backup settings. Store purchase history is managed by Apple or Google.

Children’s privacy

Italian Bros does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Advertising and store services operate under their own policies and platform controls.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will appear here. Questions can be sent to ciminillo@italianbrosco.com.
