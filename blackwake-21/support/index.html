How to play

Tap Hit to draw a card or Stand to keep your total and let the dealer play. Aces count as 11 unless counting them as 1 prevents a bust. The dealer stands on every 17.

Start another round

After a win, loss, or push, tap Deal Again to start a fresh hand.

No bets or purchases

Blackwake 21 is a just-for-fun blackjack game. It has no real-money wagering, virtual currency, purchases, accounts, ads, or prizes.

Something not working?

Close and reopen the app, then try a fresh round. If the issue continues, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened before the problem.
