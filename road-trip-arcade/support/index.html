What is Road Trip Arcade?

It is a collection of five road-trip activities designed for passengers and groups: Plate Quest, Road Bingo, Trivia Turbo, Word Race, and a randomized Mystery Game launcher.

Saving progress

Scores, streaks, crew profiles, game history, plate sightings, and the active bingo card save automatically on your device.

Resetting a game

Plate Quest and Road Bingo include their own reset or new-card controls. To clear all app progress, open Crew, scroll to Settings, and choose Reset all trip data.

Gameplay tips

  • Plate Quest tracks each U.S. state once per trip.
  • Road Bingo awards a win for any complete row, column, or diagonal.
  • Trivia Turbo and Word Race award points when a round finishes.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when something looks wrong.
