What the app collects

Road Trip Arcade does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data services.

Information stored on your device

Crew names, game progress, scores, streaks, completed-game history, bingo cards, plate sightings, and app settings are stored locally on your device so games can resume between trips. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Network access

The app's games work without an online account or cloud service. It does not upload your gameplay or crew information.

Deleting your data

You can reset Road Trip Arcade data from the app's Crew and Settings screen. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or adults because it does not transmit personal data to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
