Rooted support

Rooted In God's Word is in TestFlight testing with a temporary interface. The connected beta starts at version 1.0.0 (3). Internal and public availability depend on the build offered by TestFlight; this page is not a download invitation.

Start studying

Start a guest profile and open the included Philippians course. Build 3 includes NLT Scripture, commentary-based teaching and draft learning checklists, so no import is required. Earlier builds 1 and 2 require a compatible private pack.

In Library, My content packs also accepts an authored Rooted study-pack JSON file smaller than 50 MB. Ordinary Bible JSON, PDFs and audio files require conversion first. Use material you are entitled to use. Importing does not change your selected translation.

Practice and compare

Choose a short set. Type, recall silently or aloud, or ask a nearby listener to check. Finish recalling before revealing the supplied comparison. Words, meaning and references use different checks. For meaning, compare every supplied essential and qualification. The included checklists are drafts for learner or listener comparison, with no automatic theological judgment.

Visible help stays marked as assistance. Success during practice differs from a later recall check. Progress reports the checks you completed; it does not measure spiritual maturity. Optional recording is for your own playback, without automatic speech grading.

Pause and return

Use Save and leave from the stop options. Resume through Learn and Saved activities. Completed checks stay saved. Resuming an interrupted response does not make it an uninterrupted recital.

Accounts and cloud progress

Guest study works locally. An optional adult account lets you synchronize supported learning records after signing in and verifying your email. Choose the guest-to-account merge explicitly when you want to bring guest learning into the account. On another device, use the same account and source revision.

Notes, bookmarks, imported source prose, unfinished responses and recordings are not all synchronized. Save a backup of personal material you need. A pending operation is not a confirmed server action. If offline, keep the app's local records and retry after reconnecting.

Private groups and cooperative practice

When the service has active moderation coverage, create or join an invited private group, accept the displayed sharing policy, and select a goal or activity. Your answers and accuracy remain private; shared screens show participation and completion. A cooperative activity needs contributions from at least two people.

A room's unfinished assignment can expire or be released. Your personal learning remains saved even if a shared tile changes. Completed history is available only to eligible participants and expires after 30 days. Use the relevant block or report action if needed. Groups and in-app support show their availability in the app.

Import, save or audio problems

Keep your original import file. An import error should leave existing study data intact. If saving fails, keep the app open if possible, read its recovery message, and use Retry or export pending work. Free device storage if it reports being full. Avoid clearing app data while recovering an unsaved change.

For listening, check output volume and installed device voices. Microphone recording and local reminders are optional; change permissions in device settings. Typing, self-checking and listener comparison remain available.

Back up or delete

Readable export may contain private notes and imported material, so choose its destination carefully. Backup preview and merge require a compatible backup for the same profile and preserve a separate pre-restore copy. A backup cannot replace source answers or grant new distribution rights.

Use account deletion after recent sign-in to request removal of cloud account data. The app retains a private status receipt so you can check completion after a lost connection or restart. A queued or failed request is not confirmed erasure. Guest-profile erasure is a separate command; exports and third-party copies must be handled separately.

Report a problem

Use the available in-app case form, TestFlight feedback or email ciminillo@italianbrosco.com. Include app build, device and operating-system version, screen name, steps and observed result. A short reproduction with nonprivate text is useful.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, full licensed packs, private recordings or personal notes. Review screenshots before sharing them. Read the privacy notice for details.
