Privacy

Updated October 7, 2026. This notice covers the connected beta of Rooted In God's Word, version 1.0.0 starting with build 3. Builds 1 and 2 provide local guest study only. Italian Bros operates Rooted.

Guest study and local records

You can study Bible Foundations or Philippians without creating an account. Guest names, notes, bookmarks, content packs, learning records, preferences and unfinished activities are stored in the app's local data. Older learning records may contain typed responses. Study records and personal reflections can reveal religious interests.

Importing a file does not publish it or grant redistribution rights. Imported source text, commentary and recordings stay on your device. Guest notes and bookmarks stay local until you create an account or choose to add your guest journey to an existing account. Keep a separate backup of local material you want to preserve.

Optional accounts and synchronization

When you choose to create an adult account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and authentication credentials. Rooted receives your account identifier, display name, adult declaration, time zone and profile changes. Email verification and account recovery are handled through Firebase Authentication.

Cloud synchronization sends learning target and source identifiers, timing, practice method, assistance, comparison hashes and progression receipts. Choice games send question and selected-option identifiers so the server can check answers. Raw typed answers from older activities and source prose are removed from automatic synchronization. Saved progress determines suggested repair and review activities. This study history may reveal religious interests and is linked to your account.

The redesigned beta also saves your private notes, bookmarks, chosen path, lesson position, supported preferences, helper name, earned items and home layout to your account. These records let you return on another device. Private notes are not shared with group members. Earlier builds that do not support this restoration keep notes and bookmarks on the device.

Creating an account from a guest journey carries that journey into the new account. Adding guest learning to an existing account requires a review and an explicit import. If two devices edit the same record, Rooted keeps the conflicting versions for you to resolve. A record that exceeds the supported size remains on the device with a saving issue. Imported source files, recordings and device permissions are not part of account restoration. Guest study remains available without signing in.

Private groups and shared activities

When private groups are enabled and you choose to participate, Rooted processes group and goal names, memberships, display names, roles, consent decisions, preset encouragement and cooperative activity state. Other participants can see participation and completion. Private answers and accuracy are not included in the shared room state.

Completed room history is limited by current membership, original participation and blocks. Completed rooms expire after 30 days; unfinished live rooms after two hours and asynchronous rooms after seven days. Removal is processed by a scheduled cleanup task. Block and report controls are available in the relevant group or room screens.

Support and reports

If you submit an in-app support request, content issue or abuse report, we receive the text you choose to send and subsequent replies. If you choose to include diagnostics, they are limited to the app version, platform, error code and queue count. Authorized operators can read and handle these cases. Availability of in-app intake depends on an active response channel.

If you email Italian Bros, we use your address and correspondence to respond. Do not send passwords, verification codes, private recordings or full licensed study packs. You can request access to or removal of developer-held support correspondence at the address below.

Recording, listening and reminders

Microphone permission is optional and requested when you choose to record. Recordings are for playback and human comparison on your device. Rooted does not upload, transcribe or automatically grade them. Temporary recordings are intended to be discarded when you leave the activity; an interruption or storage failure may delay cleanup.

Listening uses the operating system's speech service and available voices, rather than licensed narrator audio. Voice downloads and device speech-service behavior are controlled by the operating system. Practice reminders are optional local notifications and can be disabled in app or device settings.

Backups and exports

A readable export may contain private notes, typed answers, imported material and learning records. You choose its destination. A sharing or storage service handles its copy under its own policies. Backup import validates compatible records for the same profile and keeps a local pre-restore safety copy. Deletion-status secrets and authentication credentials are excluded from readable exports.

Device backups, exported files and email copies are handled separately. Uninstalling Rooted or deleting a Rooted account does not remove copies you shared elsewhere.

Providers, retention and testing

Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, database and server processing. These services process ordinary network and request information needed to deliver and secure Rooted. Default operational cloud logs are retained for 30 days; required Google Cloud audit logs are retained for 400 days. Those provider logs are separate from the study records in your account.

Active account learning records remain available until account deletion. Account deletion removes the account's stored study records, membership and private room records, and submitted cases, and removes its identity links from shared activity records. Shared aggregate completion can remain. A temporary barrier prevents late requests from recreating deleted data, and a limited deletion-status receipt remains for 30 days. Deletion is asynchronous: a queued request is not a completed deletion.

Rooted has no ads, cross-app tracking, purchased audience data, payment checkout or third-party analytics SDK. These information pages have no advertising or analytics scripts; their host may process IP address, page and request time to serve and secure the website.

Apple operates TestFlight distribution. Apple collects beta crash and usage information and shares it, along with feedback you submit, with the developer. We use it to investigate problems and improve Rooted. Apple controls records in its own service. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Your controls

You can edit or remove notes and bookmarks, remove a pack's local source content, change preferences, export accessible records, sign out, and erase a guest profile. Removing a pack preserves its learning history and related notes. Removing a guest profile also removes its local pre-restore safety copy.

For an account, use the account-deletion command after recent sign-in. Keep the app installed while checking its saved deletion status. An accepted deletion continues on the server even if you disconnect. If the app cannot confirm completion, keep its recovery information and contact support; do not interpret a network error as cancellation.
