Privacy

Effective October 6, 2026. This notice describes Rooted In God's Word 1.0.0, internal TestFlight builds 1 and 2. These builds provide local guest study. Rooted cloud accounts, group services and in-app support delivery are not enabled.

Your study on this device

Your guest display name, imported study packs, notes, bookmarks, preferences, learning attempts, exposure history and unfinished activities are stored in the app's local data. Typed responses may be retained with a learning record or unfinished activity. These records can reveal the passages you study and personal reflections.

Importing a file does not publish it or grant redistribution rights. The app uses the file you select to display source text, teaching, prompts and supplied answer checks. In this build, it does not upload the imported pack or your study records to a Rooted server.

Recording and listening

Microphone permission is optional and requested when you choose to record an attempt. Recordings are for playback and human comparison on the device. The app does not upload, transcribe or automatically grade them. Temporary recordings are intended to be discarded when you leave the activity; an interruption or storage failure may delay cleanup.

Listening uses the operating system's speech service and available voices. It is not licensed narrator audio. Voice downloads and device speech-service behavior are controlled by the operating system.

Reminders and exported files

Practice reminders are optional local notifications. You can disable them in app preferences or device settings. The import picker gives access to the file you choose.

A readable export can include private notes, typed answers, imported material and learning records. You choose where to save or share it. A selected sharing service handles its copy under its own policies. Build 1 does not offer restoration from this export. Build 2 adds validated same-profile backup merging and keeps a pre-restore safety copy on the device. A backup can contain imported source material and personal notes, so keep it private.

Online services and testing

This build contains no enabled Rooted cloud account, multiplayer service, advertising, analytics reporting or payment checkout. Unavailable controls have not transmitted a support case or created an online account. A future build that enables online processing will require an updated notice describing that processing.

Apple operates TestFlight distribution under its own terms. During beta testing, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you submit is also shared. We use this information to investigate problems and improve Rooted, and retain it as needed for that purpose. Contact us about access or deletion of developer-held feedback; Apple controls the records in its own service. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Support and this website

If you email Italian Bros, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request and maintain the support correspondence. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private recordings or full licensed study packs. You may contact us to request access to or removal of your support correspondence.

These information pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, requested page and request time, to serve and secure the website.

Your controls

You can edit or remove notes and bookmarks, remove an imported pack's local source content, change preferences and export accessible local records. Removing a pack preserves its learning history and related notes. Build 2 also provides an explicit erase-guest-profile command, which removes that guest profile and its local pre-restore safety copy. Build 1 does not have this command.

Device app-management controls can remove the app's local data. Copies in device backups, shared exports and email are handled separately by those services. We do not promise that removing the app deletes those copies.

Contact and changes

For questions or requests concerning Rooted data, email ciminillo@italianbrosco.com. This notice will identify changes when new processing is enabled.
