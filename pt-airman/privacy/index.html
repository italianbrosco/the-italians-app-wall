What the app collects

PT Airman does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or tracking.

Fitness data

Your profile, assessment results, altitude setting, and history are stored locally on your device. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Network access

The core scoring, training, and history features work offline. The app can open official guidance and the Italian Bros website when you choose a link. A future signed-rules update service may check for reviewed scoring data; it will not upload your profile or fitness results.

Deleting your data

You can clear local history in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not transmit personal fitness data to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
