Game help and technical support

How do I play?

Drag across the arena to set the ball’s direction, then release to launch. Use wall bounces to clear every target before your shots run out.

What do the targets mean?

  • Standard targets clear in one hit.
  • Armored targets need more than one hit.
  • Blast targets can trigger a chain reaction.

Where is my progress?

Unlocked rooms and best scores are saved locally on your device. Use Reset Progress from the menu only when you want to start over.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen the app if the arena, audio, or haptics stop responding.
  • Check the app menu to confirm sound and haptics are enabled.
  • Make sure the latest testing build is installed before reporting a problem.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, room number, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording is helpful.
