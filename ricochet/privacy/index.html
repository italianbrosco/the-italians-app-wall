Effective August 25, 2026

Data collection

Ricochet! does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party sign-in.

What stays on your device

Unlocked rooms, best scores, and sound and haptics preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to restore your game progress and settings.

Permissions and device features

The app can play bundled sound effects and use device haptics for game feedback. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifier.

Purchases and advertising

Ricochet! has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.

Deleting your data

Use Reset Progress in the app to clear saved game progress and settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children’s privacy

Because Ricochet! does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed behavior is released.
