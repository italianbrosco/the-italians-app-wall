ABC Smash

Little learners.
Private play.

Effective August 15, 2026

What stays on the device

ABC Smash does not require an account. Optional local profile names, settings, letter progress and history, bug collections, purchase entitlement status, and a randomly generated local device identifier are stored on the device. They are not uploaded to an Italian Bros account or server.

Analytics and advertising

No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included. ABC Smash does not track children, build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or share child, profile, or gameplay data with third parties.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional lifetime upgrade through the App Store on iPhone and iPad. ABC Smash receives only the product and purchase status needed to unlock or restore the upgrade. Italian Bros does not receive or store payment-card details. The web version does not sell the upgrade.

Device permissions

ABC Smash does not collect location, contacts, photos, camera images, microphone recordings, or speech recordings.

Children’s privacy

The game opens directly into local child play. Grown-up settings and purchases are behind a parent math gate. Because gameplay data remains local, Italian Bros does not knowingly receive personal information from children.

Deleting data

Use the grown-up reset controls to clear local progress, or delete the app to remove its locally stored data, subject to the device’s backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page before the new practice takes effect.
