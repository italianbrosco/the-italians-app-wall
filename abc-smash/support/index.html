Quick help for little learners and their grown-ups.

How to play

Tap the bug’s covering two or three times to reveal it. Listen for the letter, then follow and tap the walking bug until it is squashed. Choose Next letter to continue.

Sound not playing?

Turn up the device volume, make sure Silent Mode is off, close and reopen ABC Smash, and try the letter again. Letter and word prompts are bundled with the app and do not require an account.

Grown-up controls

Tap the people control four times, then answer the worded math question. This opens local profile, progress, reset, and purchase controls without exposing them during normal child play.

Restore the lifetime upgrade

Open the grown-up upgrade screen and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. The lifetime upgrade is not a subscription.

Still stuck?

Include the iPhone model, iOS version, the letter or screen involved, and what happened just before the problem.
