FAMILY CATECHISM · PRIVACY POLICY

Your family’s learning
stays on your device.

Effective September 20, 2026

Information in the app

Family Catechism stores what a parent or guardian enters while using it: a family display name; each member’s first name, adult or child profile type, avatar and color; a child’s estimated age; the selected catechism path; lesson and review history; and reminder and app preferences. This information is kept on the device with Apple-provided storage. There is no account system, and Italian Bros Co LLC does not operate a server that receives your family’s data.

What we do not collect

The app has no advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking or third-party SDKs. It does not access location, contacts, photos, the camera or the microphone, and it does not send crash reports or usage data to us.

Free access

Every lesson, family profile, review tool and family game is included. The app offers no purchases or subscriptions.

Scripture and website links

Lessons and Scripture references work offline. Tapping a Scripture reference opens that passage on ESV.org in your browser; the website receives the passage reference and ordinary web-request information under its own privacy policy. The link to our other apps opens the App Store. No family names or progress are included in any link.

Reminders

An optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on the device after you allow notifications. Its text never includes a family member’s name or progress.

Printing, sharing and exports

Lesson cards you print or share contain the catechism question, answer and Scripture references only, with no family profile or progress. “Export family progress” creates a readable summary with the family name, the selected path, each member’s first name, a child’s estimated age and a progress summary. Nothing is sent unless you choose a destination in the system share sheet, and that destination handles it under its own terms.

Children and families

A parent or guardian sets up and leads the app. Children do not create accounts, communicate with other users or send information to us. You may use a nickname instead of a legal first name.

Backups and deletion

Your device may include app data in backups or device transfers, depending on your Apple settings. “Erase all family data” in Settings removes local profiles, progress and preferences and cancels the reminder. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to those backup settings. Because we never receive your family’s data, we cannot view or erase it remotely.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, we will post the revised policy and its effective date here and update the app’s privacy details before releasing that version.
