Help with lessons, family profiles, review and your data.

Getting started

Create your family profile, then add parents, children and other family members from the Family tab. Choose a starting path in Learn: A Baptist Catechism, the 1689 London Baptist Confession, or a twelve-lesson starter collection. You can change paths at any time without losing progress.

Lessons

Each lesson walks through the question and answer, supporting Scripture references, an explanation for each family member’s age, a discussion question, a prayer, memory practice and an optional activity. Longer readings can be shared across several days. Tap a Scripture reference to read it in the ESV on ESV.org; online reading needs an internet connection, but lessons and references work offline.

Review

Review brings back each person’s learned questions just before they are likely to be forgotten. Practice with flashcards or the cooperative family game. There are no daily streaks or attendance targets.

Print or share a lesson

Open any lesson and tap Print or Share. The one-page card includes the question, the answer and every Scripture reference, but no family names or progress.

Daily reminder

Turn on the reminder in Settings and choose a time. If it doesn’t appear, allow notifications for Family Catechism in your device’s Settings app.

Your data

Family profiles and progress stay on your device. Use Settings to export a progress summary or erase all family data. Your device’s backup settings decide whether app data is included in backups and device transfers.

Content questions and corrections

If you find a source or Scripture reference that looks wrong, email us with the collection and question or paragraph number so we can review it carefully.

Something not working?

Email us with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the app version shown at the bottom of Settings, and the step that failed.
