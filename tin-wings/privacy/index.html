What the app collects

Tin Wings does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party marketing services.

Information stored on your device

Game settings and preferences are stored locally on your device so they remain available between flights. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Online multiplayer

When you create or join a private online room, the game sends temporary room and gameplay state to the Tin Wings multiplayer service so connected players can participate in the same match. Room codes and live match state are used only to operate the session and are not used for advertising or user profiling.

Deleting your data

You can change or reset preferences in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings. Temporary online room data expires when the session ends.

Children's privacy

Tin Wings does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
