What the app collects

Tin Wings does not require an account. Italian Bros does not use third-party analytics and does not build advertising profiles. The app offers optional rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads; an ad is requested only after you choose a clearly labeled reward action.

Optional rewarded ads

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, general device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve rewarded ads. Where required, Tin Wings asks for your privacy choices before requesting an ad. You can decline the ad and continue playing; coins are granted only after Google reports that the voluntary reward was completed.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy. You can revisit available advertising privacy choices from Tin Wings settings.

Information stored on your device

Game settings, career kills, coins, unlocked planes and pilots, free-gift timing, and reward history are stored locally on your device so they remain available between flights. Italian Bros does not receive this progression information.

Online multiplayer

When you create, find, or join an online room, the game sends your chosen pilot name plus temporary room and gameplay state to the Tin Wings multiplayer service so connected players can participate in the same match. Room codes and live match state are used only to operate the session and are not used for advertising or user profiling.

Deleting your data

You can change or reset preferences in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored progression and settings, subject to your device backup settings. Temporary online room data expires when the session ends. Advertising choices can be changed through the privacy-options control when Google makes it available for your region.

Children's privacy

Tin Wings is not directed to children under 13. Italian Bros does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an online pilot name or support request, contact us so it can be addressed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
