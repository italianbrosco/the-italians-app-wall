One More

Push your luck.
Keep your privacy.

Effective August 25, 2026

Local game data

One More stores your runs, saves, highest save, click and bust history, badges, selected friends, live-view preferences, and active rewarded-ad benefit on your device. This keeps your personal stats and game progress available between sessions.

Accounts and shared stats

An account is optional. If you create one, Italian Bros processes your unique username, a securely salted and hashed version of your password, session information, scores, aggregate game stats, and the usernames you choose to look up as friends. The service never stores your plain-text password. Signed-in stats may appear on global and friends-only rankings under your username.

Live friends

When you are signed in on the Home tab, the service briefly publishes your current score or bust state so friends who have chosen to watch you can see it. This presence expires automatically within seconds after updates stop.

Advertising and consent

After a bust, you may choose to watch a Google rewarded ad for a temporary in-game risk reduction. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, consent choices, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Service operation

Italian Bros processes network and request information as needed to deliver account, leaderboard, friends, abuse-prevention, and security features. The app does not sell scores, account credentials, or friends lists.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes locally stored game data, subject to your device backup settings. You can permanently delete an optional One More account and its associated server profile, sessions, live presence, leaderboard entries, and shared stats from the Stats tab in the app or through the One More account deletion page. An anonymized aggregate global-best value may remain because it is no longer linked to an account.

Children’s privacy

Italian Bros does not knowingly collect personal information from children through One More. Advertising and platform services operate under their own policies and account controls.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will appear here. Questions can be sent to ciminillo@italianbrosco.com.
