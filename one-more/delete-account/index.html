What will be deleted

Your One More account profile, password hash, sessions, live presence, shared stats, and account-linked leaderboard entries will be permanently removed. Local game stats stay on each device unless you also delete the app or clear its storage. An anonymized aggregate global-best value may remain because it is not linked to an account.
