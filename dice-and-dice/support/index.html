Add and remove dice

Use the minus and plus controls for D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, D100, and Coin. Mix the types you need, then use Clear to empty the tray.

Choose a roll style

3D Roll sends the dice tumbling and bouncing around the wooden tray before gathering them together. 2D Spin keeps each die in its place while it spins to a result.

Roll again

Tap Roll Dice or tap the wooden tray. You can roll again while dice are moving. The result strip shows every die and the total; the coin reports Heads or Tails.

D100 results

The D100 is one physical 100-face die. Its result is included directly in the total like every other numbered die.

Something not working?

Close and reopen the app, then try a two-dice roll in each mode. If the issue continues, include your device model, operating-system version, selected dice, mode, and what happened before the problem.
