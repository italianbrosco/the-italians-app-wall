What the app collects

Dice & Dice: Best Dice does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party data services.

Dice and roll state

Your selected dice, roll results, physics state, and display mode are handled on your device while the app is open. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Motion and audio

The app may respond to local device motion as a roll gesture and plays locally generated sound effects. Motion readings and audio are not recorded, uploaded, or shared.

Network access

The dice roller works without an account or cloud service. The released app does not send gameplay or device information to Italian Bros.

Deleting your data

Closing the app ends the current roll session. Deleting the app removes the app and any locally managed system data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or adults because it does not transmit personal data to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
