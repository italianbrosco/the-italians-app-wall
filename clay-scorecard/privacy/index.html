Yawtzee: Best Scorecard

Privacy,
kept simple.

Effective August 21, 2026

What the app processes

Yawtzee: Best Scorecard does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. The app uses an anonymous technical identifier to connect securely to its shared-scorecard service.

Information stored on your device

Player names, scores, completed-game history, statistics, and app settings are stored locally on your device so the scorecard can work and resume your games.

Shared scorecards

When you create or join a scorecard, its game number, game title, entered player names, scores, current-player selection, bonus counts, and update timestamps are transmitted to and stored in Google Firebase. This lets people using the same invite link see and update the same scorecard. Do not enter information you do not want other people with the invite link to see.

Shared scorecards are not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling. They remain available so the invite continues to work. To request deletion of a shared scorecard, email us with its six-digit game number.

Sharing

If you choose the app’s Share feature, your device opens its standard share sheet. You decide what to share, which service receives it, and whether to complete the action. Anyone with a scorecard invite can access that shared scorecard. Other sharing services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can remove completed games in the app and reset local app data from Settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings, but does not delete a shared scorecard from Firebase. Contact us with the six-digit game number to request deletion of that shared scorecard.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for families, but shared scorecards should not contain sensitive personal information. We do not knowingly use children’s information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
