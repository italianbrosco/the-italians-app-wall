Privacy,

Effective August 10, 2026

What the app collects

Clay Scorecard does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

Player names, scores, completed-game history, statistics, and app settings are stored locally on your device so the scorecard can work and resume your games. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Sharing

If you choose the app’s Share feature, your device opens its standard share sheet. You decide what to share, which service receives it, and whether to complete the action. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can remove completed games in the app and reset local app data from Settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or adults because it does not transmit personal data to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
