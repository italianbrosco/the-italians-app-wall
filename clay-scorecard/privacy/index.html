Yawtzee: Best Scorecard

Privacy,
kept simple.

Effective August 25, 2026

What the app processes

Yawtzee: Best Scorecard does not require an account. It uses Google Firebase for shared scorecards, Google Mobile Ads for occasional full-screen ads, and Apple App Store or Google Play Billing for the optional Ad-Free Access subscription.

Information stored on your device

Player names, scores, completed-game history, statistics, and app settings are stored locally on your device so the scorecard can work and resume your games.

Shared scorecards

When you create or join a scorecard, its game number, game title, entered player names, scores, current-player selection, bonus counts, and update timestamps are transmitted to and stored in Google Firebase. This lets people using the same invite link see and update the same scorecard. Do not enter information you do not want other people with the invite link to see.

Shared scorecards are not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling. They remain available so the invite continues to work. To request deletion of a shared scorecard, email us with the invite link, game title, and player names needed to identify it.

Advertising

The free experience may show a Google Mobile Ads interstitial when you start, resume, or join a scorecard. Active Ad-Free Access subscribers do not receive these ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and platform rules, Google Mobile Ads may process device or other identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising data to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. The app requests consent where required and does not sell your shared-scorecard content to advertisers.

Google describes its mobile ads data practices in its iOS and Android disclosures.

Ad-Free Access purchases

Subscription purchases and renewals are handled by Apple or Google. Italian Bros receives the product identifier and entitlement status needed to remove ads, but does not receive your complete payment-card information. Manage or cancel the subscription through your App Store or Google Play account.

Sharing

If you choose the app’s Share feature, your device opens its standard share sheet. You decide what to share, which service receives it, and whether to complete the action. Anyone with a scorecard invite can access that shared scorecard. Other sharing services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can remove completed games in the app and reset local app data from Settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings, but does not delete a shared scorecard from Firebase. Contact us with the invite link, game title, and player names needed to identify and delete that shared scorecard.

Children’s privacy

The store listing is intended for ages 13 and older. Shared scorecards should not contain sensitive personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
