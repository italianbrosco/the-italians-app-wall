Help with scoring, saved games, settings, and sharing.

What is Yawtzee?

It is a companion score sheet for games played with five physical dice. The app validates score entries, totals every category, and keeps completed-game history. It is not a simulated game; the optional dice sandbox is available only when your table needs dice.

Start a game

Choose New Scorecard, add the players at your table, and begin. On each turn, choose the player, select a scoring category, and enter the result shown by the physical dice.

Resume or correct a score

An active game is saved automatically. Reopen the app to continue. Tap a filled category to change or reset that square.

Shared scorecards

Use the share button to invite another device. The invite link opens the same live scorecard, while each device keeps its own selected player and screen view.

Ads and Ad-Free Access

The free experience may show a full-screen ad when starting, resuming, or joining a scorecard. Settings offers optional monthly and yearly Ad-Free Access subscriptions, plus Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription.

History and statistics

Completed games appear in History and contribute to the local statistics shown in the app. You can remove games or reset local data from Settings.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when something looks wrong.
