What is Clay Scorecard?

It is a companion score sheet for games played with five physical dice. The app tracks turns, validates score entries, totals every category, and keeps completed-game history. It does not roll dice or simulate gameplay.

Start a game

Choose New Game, add the players at your table, and begin. On each turn, select a scoring category and enter the result shown by the physical dice. The app advances to the next player automatically.

Resume or undo

An active game is saved automatically on your device. Reopen the app to continue, or use Undo immediately after an entry to correct the latest score.

History and statistics

Completed games appear in History and contribute to the local statistics shown in the app. You can remove games or reset local data from Settings.

Contact support

Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when something looks wrong.
