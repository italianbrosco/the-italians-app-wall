Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Offline lessons and games

The app bundles the original lessons and games from all three discs. Playing does not require an account or an Internet connection. The app has no advertising, purchases, tracking, or developer analytics service. It does not transmit lesson activity or personal information to Italian Bros through an app backend.

Information on your device

App settings and game data, including the last lesson opened, may be stored locally on your device. Italian Bros does not automatically receive this local data. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to your device backup settings.

Printing

If you choose to print a coloring page, the app passes the displayed page to the iOS or iPadOS printing system. System printing may communicate with the printer you choose.

TestFlight beta testing

Apple automatically collects beta crash logs and usage information through TestFlight and shares information with the app developer. Feedback you submit can include your comments and screenshots. We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate problems and improve this app, and do not share that TestFlight information with third parties. Apple controls its collection and retention under TestFlight & Privacy. Contact us for questions or access/deletion requests about beta feedback shared with us.

Children

The app does not request personal information from children or send their lesson activity to us. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy or support. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.
