Help with your round, scores and card deck.

Start a round

Choose 9 or 18 holes, add up to eight players and select a scoring format. Deal one card for the group or one per player. Use Course setup to enter par and stroke-index information for your tees.

Scores and handicaps

Enter strokes played separately from card adjustments. Use a Course Handicap for the selected tees and round length, rather than a Handicap Index. Stroke play, Stableford and two-player match play use the scores and settings you enter.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases in Settings while signed into the same Apple or Google store account that made the purchase. A purchase belongs to its original store and is not automatically transferred between Apple and Google.

Saved rounds

Round data is saved on the device. Keep the app installed to retain local history. Your device’s backup settings determine whether app data is included in backups.

Play responsibly

Follow course rules and respect other players. Skip any card you do not want to play. Some cards contain drinking prompts; participation is optional. Never drink and drive.

Something not working?

Email us with your device model, operating-system version, app version and the step that failed. For a purchase issue, include the store and a description, but never send passwords or full payment details.
