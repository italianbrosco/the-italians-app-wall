MULLIGAN MADNESS · PRIVACY POLICY

Your round stays
on your phone.

Effective October 31, 2026

Information in the app

Mulligan Madness stores your player names, course settings, scores, card choices, preferences and round history locally on your device. You do not need an account. Italian Bros Co LLC does not receive this round data through an app server.

Purchases

An optional one-time purchase unlocks the full card deck. Apple or Google processes purchases and manages payment information under its own privacy policy. The app uses the store’s purchase status to provide and restore the unlock. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.

Backups and deletion

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Removing the app deletes its local data, subject to those backup settings. Purchase history remains with the store that processed it.

Support and websites

If you email us, we receive the message and the information you choose to include so we can help. Do not send payment-card details or passwords. Opening a website or the app store is subject to that service’s policies.

Audience and changes

Some game cards refer to drinking. The game is intended for an appropriate adult audience. We will post any revised privacy policy and its effective date here.
