Game help and technical support

How do I play?

Draw angled crayon walls in the playground. Walls bounce incoming projectiles and redirect charging doodles. Protect Pip without running out of blue crayon.

What is Today’s Doodle?

Today’s Doodle uses the same daily challenge for everyone. Your daily best and personal best stay on your device.

Why did a wall disappear?

Every wall has limited strength and lifetime. Use shorter, well-placed lines and watch the blue-crayon meter before drawing again.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen the app if drawing, sound, or haptics stop responding.
  • Confirm the latest testing build is installed before reporting an issue.
  • On Android, make sure no accessibility or overlay app is intercepting touch gestures.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot or short screen recording is helpful.
