Effective August 25, 2026

Data collection

Draw Your Defense does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party sign-in, or a remote gameplay service.

What stays on your device

Your personal best, daily-run best, and sound preference are stored locally on your device so the game can restore them between sessions.

Permissions and network access

The game is bundled inside the app and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifier. The Android package includes standard internet capability required by its embedded web-view framework, but Draw Your Defense does not use it to send gameplay or personal data to Italian Bros.

Purchases and advertising

Draw Your Defense has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app’s storage or deleting the app removes its locally stored game data, subject to your device and platform backup settings.

Children’s privacy

The app has no account, messaging, user-generated content, or mechanism for collecting information from children or other players.
