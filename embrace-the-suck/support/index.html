Help with challenges, adaptations, progress, and saved workouts.

How challenges work

Choose a daily, weekly, or discovered challenge, select the effort level that fits today, then use the built-in move sequence and timer. Progress saves automatically on your device.

Adaptations and safety

Open a challenge’s adaptations to choose lower-impact or equipment-free alternatives. Work at your own pace, stop if a movement causes pain, and seek qualified medical guidance when appropriate.

Saved progress and badges

Completed challenges, streaks, badges, and saved workouts remain available after relaunch because they are stored locally on your device.

Crew and Global views

These views use clearly labeled sample, local-only activity in this release. They do not represent live accounts or uploaded workouts.

Sharing

Sharing opens the standard system share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and complete the share there.

Resetting local data

If you need a complete reset, delete and reinstall the app. This removes the app’s local data, subject to your device backup settings.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when something looks wrong.
