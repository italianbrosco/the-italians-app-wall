What the app collects

Embrace the Suck does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data services.

Information stored on your device

Your profile, challenge progress, completion history, streaks, badges, saved challenges, and local crew connections are stored in the app’s local database. Italian Bros does not receive this information.

Sample content

The Crew and Global views contain clearly labeled sample activity bundled with the app. This sample content is not a live social network and does not upload your activity.

Sharing

The app opens your device’s standard share sheet only when you choose to share. The destination you select is governed by that service’s privacy practices.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Health information

The app does not access Apple Health, location, contacts, photos, or other protected device data. Exercise guidance is general information and is not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
