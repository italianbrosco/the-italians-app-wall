What the app collects

Are You Human? Prove It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party marketing services. Italian Bros does not receive your answers, drawings, timing, or scores.

Information stored on your device

Your active run, completed results, daily result, and game history are stored locally on your device so you can resume and revisit your latest verdict.

Sharing

If you choose Share the Evidence, your device opens its standard share sheet with a short score summary. You decide which service receives it and whether to complete the action. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its locally stored game data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
