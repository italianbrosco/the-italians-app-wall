How do I play?

Choose Prove It for a twelve-test run, Daily Human Test for today’s shared sequence, or Practice Being Human to focus on one type of challenge. Read each prompt, respond, and let the robot judge you.

Why did my confidence drop?

The game considers your answer, reaction time, tap rhythm, drawing geometry, and suspiciously perfect behavior. Being fast or accurate is useful—but being too perfect can look robotic.

Can I resume a run?

Yes. Use Pause or Save & Exit. The home screen offers Resume Run when an unfinished test sequence is stored on your device.

My drawing will not submit

Draw one continuous shape with enough movement for the robot to inspect it. Clear the canvas and try again if the Judge My Circle button remains unavailable.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen the app; an active run should restore automatically.
  • Confirm your device is running a current supported operating-system version.
  • If sharing does not open, verify that sharing services are available on the device.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, the test prompt, and what happened. A screenshot is helpful when something looks suspicious.
