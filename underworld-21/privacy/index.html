What the app collects

Underworld 21 does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party data services.

Gameplay data

Cards, round results, and the session win-loss record exist only while you play. The app does not upload or share this gameplay information.

Network access

The game works without an online account or cloud service. Italian Bros does not receive information about your device or play.

Deleting your data

Closing the app clears the current session. Deleting the app removes the app and any locally managed system data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or adults because it does not transmit personal data to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
