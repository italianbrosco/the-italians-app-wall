What the app collects

Marbles does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party marketing services.

Gameplay information

Matches, computer-player decisions, hints, reactions, and settings are handled on your device. Italian Bros does not receive this gameplay information.

Network access

The current version does not provide online multiplayer, cloud accounts, or live player chat and does not transmit game sessions to Italian Bros.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its locally stored information, subject to your device and backup settings.

Children's privacy

Marbles does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
