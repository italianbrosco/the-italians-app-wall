What the app collects

Marbles has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party marketing services, and needs no account. Solo games and the Daily Puzzle stay on your device; Italian Bros does not receive them.

Online play

If you choose Online play, Marbles creates an anonymous player ID through Google Firebase. You don't need an email address or password. To run a match, Marbles stores your display name, avatar, piece choices and the match state in our Firebase database so the other players in your lobby can see them. All of this is sent over encrypted connections.

How long it is kept

Lobby and match records are deleted when the host leaves or closes the lobby. A lobby abandoned without being closed can remain until we remove it. To delete your online data, email ciminillo@italianbrosco.com.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its locally stored information, subject to your device and backup settings. For online data, email us as described above.

Children's privacy

Online play shows other players only the display name and avatar you choose. There is no free-text chat; quick chats and emotes are fixed phrases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
