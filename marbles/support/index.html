Help with turns, moves, hints, undo, and starting a new Family Match.

How do I start a marble?

Roll a 1 or 6 to move a marble from your base onto its starting space. Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.

How do captures work?

Land exactly on an opponent's marble to send it back to base. You cannot finish a move on a space occupied by one of your own marbles.

How do I reach home?

Move around the track into your colored home lane. The roll must fit exactly. The first player to bring all four marbles home wins.

Hints, undo, and new matches

Hint highlights a legal move when one is available. Undo restores your previous move. New Match asks for confirmation before resetting the board.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, turn number, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when something looks wrong.
